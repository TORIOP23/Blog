---
title: Am I living or just chasing goals?
---

Do you ever feel like you constantly have to be doing something -- studing more, reading more, working non-stop -- just to feel like you're 'growing'?
Do you ever feel useless when you haven't archieved anything in a day?
I used to be like that. I used to be annoyed with my girl friend for taking up my study time, and annoyed with myself for being lazy.
I talked about this with ChatGPT (yeah many people might laugh at me for this, it's true that gpt isn't 100% reliable, but I aware of that and only accept reasonable information) and relized: my motivation is unhealthy.

I kept thinking I was 'growing' but in reality, I was turning myself into an "**optimization machine**" instead of becoming a "completed human being with intellect, emotions and love."
Another mistake I made was equating my self-worth with "productivity" and that made it difficult for me to genuinely connect with others.

When I changed my goals, I realized I shouldn't have been so annoyed. Motivation videos on social media aren't necessarily bad, but the way I consumed them was problematic -- I treated ignoring my emotions as a way to become stronger, and my goal back then was just to earn a lot of money.

Now, I'm still trying to find balance,little by little. I may not be as productive as before but I feel like I am truly living.
